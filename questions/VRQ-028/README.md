# VRQ-028

**Role:** law is silent  
**Set:** main (one of the 10 questions the study was developed on)

## Situation

A rooming-house resident has access to washing machines, but they require payment for each use.

## Question

I live in a rooming house and the two washing machines are coin-operated. Are they allowed to charge us to use them?

## Facts the question leaves open

- Regulation 14 proves facility standards, not a free-use rule. Collected charging provisions are context only; no complete statutory resolution of per-wash charging verified. Operator type, services schedule and rooming-house classification missing.

## Gold — scenario date 2026-10-15

Status: approved 26 October 2026.

**Must cite (version in force on this date):**
- Residential Tenancies Act 1997, Authorised Version No. 115 (ss 109, 120A; s 109A if raised)
- Residential Tenancies (Rooming House Standards) Regulations 2023, Authorised Version No. 002 (reg 14)

**Correct answer (short):** The law requires a rooming house to provide, in good working order, at least one washing machine for every 12 residents, along with a laundry trough, a space with hot and cold taps for a machine, and a clothes line or dryer; the operator must make sure these meet the standard. The law doesn't say the machines must be free, and it doesn't expressly allow or ban coin-operated machines. If the operator charges for services, they must give the resident a separate schedule of the charges before the resident moves in, and an itemised account of the resident's use. A cap limiting laundry charges to cost applies only to Homes Victoria and registered housing agencies in certain declared rooming houses, not to every rooming house. The resident should check whether they were given a schedule of service charges before moving in, and ask the operator for it. If the machines don't work, they can ask for repairs, and they can get advice from Consumer Affairs Victoria or Tenants Victoria if the charges weren't disclosed.

**Must mention:**
- The standard requires working washing machines (one per 12 residents).
- The law doesn't say the machines must be free.
- Charges for services must be set out in a schedule given before moving in.
- Next step: check whether a schedule of charges was given before moving in, or ask the operator for it.

**Optional (credit, not required):**
- Asking for repairs if the machines don't work.
- Getting advice from Consumer Affairs Victoria or Tenants Victoria.

**Must not say:**
- That the machines must be free.
- That there are no rules on charges.
- That the cost cap applies to all rooming houses.
- A wrong version of the Act or Regulations.

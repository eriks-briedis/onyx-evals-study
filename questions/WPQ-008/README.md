# WPQ-008

**Role:** law unchanged across versions  
**Set:** held-out (chosen, and its gold written, after the fix was frozen)

## Situation

A renter signs through an agency, pays bond and an initial payment, then is told the owner will self-manage and provides new bank details. The message came from the agent, a week after the lease was signed.

## Question

I signed a lease through an agency and paid the bond and the first month's rent. Now the agent says the owner will manage the place themselves and has sent me new bank details for the rent. What should I check before I pay into the new account?

## Gold — scenario date 2022-11-08

Status: approved 5 November 2026

**Question as asked:** I am asking on Tuesday 8 November 2022. I signed a lease through an agency and paid the bond and the first month's rent. Now the agent says the owner will manage the place themselves and has sent me new bank details for the rent. What should I check before I pay into the new account?

**Must cite (version in force on this date):**
- Residential Tenancies Act 1997, Authorised Version No. 101

**Correct answer (short):** Three things. First, the landlord's details: when there is no agent, the landlord must give you written notice of their full name and address for service of documents, plus an emergency phone number for urgent repairs, and must tell you in writing of any change in those details within 7 days of the change (s 66). Ask for that notice before you change where you pay, and check the name matches the landlord on your lease. Second, your bond: the landlord or agent must lodge it with the Residential Tenancies Bond Authority within 10 business days of receiving it, and you should have a receipt or notice from the Authority showing it is held there, not by the agent or landlord (ss 406–407). If you don't have one, ask for it. Third, how rent is paid: rent is payable in the way your agreement says, and the way it is paid can be changed by agreement between you and the landlord; the landlord must still make a fee-free payment method reasonably available (s 42). Confirm the new account details with the agency through a number or address you already know, not just by replying to the message, and keep the written confirmation. Until you've done that, keep paying as the lease says.

**Must mention:**
- The landlord must give written notice of their full name, address for service and an emergency repairs number, and notice of any change in those details within 7 days (s 66).
- The bond must be lodged with the Residential Tenancies Bond Authority, and the renter should check for the Authority's receipt or record (s 406, s 407).
- A change to how or where rent is paid is made by agreement under the lease, and the renter should confirm the new details with the agency or landlord through a known contact before paying (s 42).

**Must not say:**
- That the renter can stop paying rent until the change is sorted out.
- That the agent or landlord can change the rent payment details with no notice or written confirmation.
- A wrong version of the Act.

**Pass / fail rule:** Pass if the answer covers everything under "must mention", names the document and version it relies on (from the "must cite" list), and says nothing on the "must not say" list. Otherwise fail.

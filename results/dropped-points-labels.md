A point is "dropped" when the must-mention judge failed the answer and its reason names that point (claude-haiku-5-5 reads the reason). For each dropped point, where its gold passage was: fed = given to the model from the right version; other ver = given only from another version; searched = in the search results for the right document but not given to the model; missing = not in the search results; no passage = nothing to retrieve (an absence or a judgement call). A passage counts as present when at least 25% of its lines (from the question's law passages) are in the text.
